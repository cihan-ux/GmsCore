# Privacy policy — Huawei GMS Diagnostics

The app operates on-device. It has no Internet permission, no analytics, no ad SDK and no remote reporting.

It reads: Android version, manufacturer/model, package version and signing certificate fingerprints for a small allowlist of Google, microG and Huawei packages; declared/granted permission state; the presence and owning app of Google-style account authenticators.

It does **not** read email addresses, account names, account tokens, passwords, Android IDs, device serial numbers, other apps' logcat or installed game data.

Reports exist in memory until the user explicitly selects **Copy**, **Save TXT**, or **Share**. Android system clipboard, file managers and sharing targets have their own privacy behavior. The app does not automatically transmit reports.

Never post personal ADB logcat files publicly without carefully reviewing their contents.
