Huawei/Samsung read-only account comparison probe 0.4
Package: dev.cihan.microg.accountprobe
Requires Android 10+; supports LineageOS Android 11.
No INTERNET permission; does not modify, remove, or sign in accounts.
If an older probe fails to update because CI uses a different
signing key, uninstall ONLY microG Account Probe, not microG.
Google account counts are scoped ONLY to this diagnostic package.
The signature-spoofing permission is a hint, NOT a definitive test.
No account names, e-mail addresses, passwords or tokens are reported.
